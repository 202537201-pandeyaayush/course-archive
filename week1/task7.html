Simple Pasta Recipe

What You Need

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • Grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Boil a pot of water and add a pinch of salt.
  2. Add the pasta and cook according to the package instructions.
  3. Be careful when handling the boiling water to avoid burns.
  4. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the garlic until lightly golden.
  5. Add the tomato sauce, salt, and black pepper, then simmer for 5 minutes.
  6. Drain the pasta and mix it with the sauce. Serve immediately for the best flavor!
  7. Top with grated Parmesan cheese and enjoy.
