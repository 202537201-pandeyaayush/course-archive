Task 01 of 06

Why semantics matter

Use elements such as <header>, <nav>, <main>, <article>, and <footer> to expose meaningful landmarks to assistive technology.

Replace “div soup”

<header>
  <nav aria-label="Main navigation">
    <a href="../index.html">Course archive</a>
  </nav>
</header>
<main id="main-content">
  <article>
    <h1>Page title</h1>
    <p>Meaningful page content.</p>
  </article>
</main>
<footer>Course archive</footer>
  • Use one clear page-level <h1>.
  • Keep heading levels in a logical order.
  • Give the document a language, for example <html lang="en">.

Practice: inspect one page in your course project and replace generic layout divs with semantic elements where appropriate.
