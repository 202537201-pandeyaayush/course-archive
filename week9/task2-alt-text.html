Alt Text.

Practice writing meaningful alternative text for informative images and empty alt text for decorative images.

← Back to all Week 9 tasks

Why alternative text matters

Alternative text communicates the purpose or information of an image to people who cannot see it. The correct text depends on the image's context, not just its appearance.

🏫

Informative image
A campus building photograph that identifies a location.

alt="Main university library building on campus"
〰️

Decorative image
A decorative divider that adds no information.

alt=""

Useful rules

  • Describe the image's purpose in its context.
  • Keep the description concise and specific.
  • Do not start with “image of” unless needed.
  • Use empty alt text for purely decorative images.
  • For complex charts, provide a nearby detailed text summary.
