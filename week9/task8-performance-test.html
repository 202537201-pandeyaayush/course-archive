Image loading strategy

For images below the fold, loading="lazy" can defer fetching until they are near the viewport. Always include width and height (or aspect-ratio) to reduce layout shifts.

<img src="gallery.webp"
  alt="Campus garden in spring"
  width="800" height="500"
  loading="lazy" decoding="async">

Do not lazy-load the main above-the-fold hero image if it is the likely LCP element. Consider fetchpriority="high" for the most important image after testing.
