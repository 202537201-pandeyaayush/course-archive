Task 06 of 06

Run an Accessibility audit

  1. Open your personal or team project website in Chrome.
  2. Open DevTools with F12 (or right-click → Inspect).
  3. Open the Lighthouse tab.
  4. Select Accessibility only for the audit category.
  5. Click Analyze page load.
  6. Record the initial Accessibility score and screenshot the report.
  7. Expand failed audits, read the details, and fix at least three actual issues.
  8. Include a skip link in your course project.
  9. Run the same audit again and capture a screenshot of the improved score.
  10. Post both screenshots and a list of the three fixes to your weekly task page / Discord as instructed.

Common issues to check

  • Missing or unhelpful image alt text.
  • Insufficient text/background contrast.
  • Form controls without accessible labels.
  • Missing lang attribute on the <html> element.
  • Interactive elements that cannot be used by keyboard.

Homework evidence checklist

  • Initial Lighthouse Accessibility screenshot
  • At least three fixes that correspond to actual failed audits
  • Skip link added to the course project
  • Final Lighthouse Accessibility screenshot
  • Short list explaining the three fixes

Do not use made-up scores or sample screenshots as evidence. Your score depends on the project URL and the real audit. The lecture slide mentions a 95 target; aim to resolve every relevant issue, while reporting the actual score you obtain.
