Homework checklist

  1. Run Chrome Lighthouse on your personal or team project site, selecting the Accessibility audit only.
  2. Capture a screenshot of the initial Accessibility score and report.
  3. Fix at least three failing audits shown by your report.
  4. Add a skip link to your course project.
  5. Run Lighthouse again and capture the improved score.
  6. Post both screenshots and a list of the three fixes to this weekly task page / class Discord as instructed.

Important: the screenshots and scores must come from your own live project. This starter does not invent audit scores or fake screenshots.
